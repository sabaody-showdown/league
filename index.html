About Us

Welcome to the Sabaody Showdown — a competitive One Piece Trading Card Game league featuring players from across Europe. We host weekly matches, streamed live on Twitch and archived on YouTube with top-tier commentary.

League Format

Sabaody Showdown is one unified league. Each player is scheduled for two matches per week, and results contribute to a single overall standings table.
